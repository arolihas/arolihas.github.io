**Research**: My interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and biotech. I'm currently focused on deep learning for immunology, including antibody language models, causal inference and biostatistics, and clinical representation learning.

**Previously**: I was at <a href="https://www.ordaos.bio">Ordaos Bio</a>, where I worked on nanobody and miniprotein design through generative protein modeling, active learning, and molecular dynamics. Before that, I was a machine learning engineer at <a href="https://www.vant.ai">VantAI</a> (now Proxima Bio) through the technical rotational program at <a href="https://www.roivant.com">Roivant Sciences</a>, working on protein-protein interaction prediction with geometric deep learning. I received my M.S. in Machine Learning and B.S. in Computer Science from Georgia Institute of Technology. I interned at IBM, Merck, Novo Nordisk, and Virginia Systems and Technology, gaining experience in software engineering, data analysis, and web development. I studied molecular biology abroad at CPE Lyon and performed research at <a href="https://me.gatech.edu/faculty/sulchek">BioMEMS</a> and <a href="https://bsel.bme.gatech.edu/">BSEL</a>.
